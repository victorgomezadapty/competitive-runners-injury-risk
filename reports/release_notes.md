# Competitive Runners research release 1.0.0

Longitudinal Training Load & Injury Risk in Competitive Runners. Analysis and software by Víctor Andrés Gómez López; original measurements by Lövdal, den Hartigh and Azzopardi, Dataverse DOI 10.34894/UWU9PV, CC0.

Includes the full data/target audit, corrected lag order, eleven notebooks, three primary validation questions, independent calibration, matched longer-history/weighting/fixed-tree sensitivities, uncertainty and selected-row review burden. Eighteen scientific-contract tests passed. A documented narrative search and fourteen bibliographic records contextualize the findings.

The attached results archive contains project-generated tables, protocols, documentation and figures. No copyrighted articles, third-party figures or complementary raw archives are included. Original data and dedicated table exports are CC0; original code/prose are MIT; source-specific rights are documented separately.

This is exploratory selected-sample research. The source cannot identify prospective daily injury incidence, clinical diagnosis, causal effects of changing training, validated event lead time or injury prevention. The website explores saved outputs and does not deploy a clinical prediction model.
